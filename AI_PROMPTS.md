# AI handoff — escrowed merch custody timeline

## Non-negotiables

1. Ordered steps: **paid → shipped → delivered → released**. A skip is **refused**.
2. Cancel only from **paid** (per product description).
3. A **transfer does not prove** any step.
4. Sample payment is **unsigned**. `executed: false`.
5. Earlier README live-product claims are not what this tree ships.

## Prompt seeds

- "paid → delivered without shipped — refuse?"
- "Why does a transfer not prove delivered?"
- "Cancel from shipped — allowed or refused?"

## Do not

- Skip steps.
- Treat an on-chain transfer as automatic step advance.
- Broadcast the sample payment from tests.
